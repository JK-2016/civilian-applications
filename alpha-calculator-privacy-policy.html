Alpha Calculator Privacy Policy

Developer: Civilian Applications
Effective date: 15 November 2026
Package: com.jk2016.alphacalculator
Contact: applicationscivil.support@gmail.com

Calculations and local storage

Alpha Calculator requires no account. Your custom formulas, function definitions, variables, memory, settings and calculation history are stored in app-private storage on your device. Civilian Applications does not operate a server that receives these calculations or formula libraries. Calculations work offline. Android cloud backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes these local records.

Advertising and consent

Version 0.5.0 and later includes a Google AdMob banner on the calculator screen, separated from the keypad and navigation. Ads and consent updates require an internet connection. Earlier previews through 0.4.4 have no advertising SDK.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses, device and account identifiers (including advertising ID and app set ID where available), app and ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The app does not request precise location, camera or microphone access. We do not attach your mathematical expressions, saved formulas, variables or history to ad requests.

The Google User Messaging Platform checks whether consent is required before ads are requested. Where applicable, a consent form is presented. Use Tools → Ad privacy options to reopen available privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Non-personalized ads may still process data. Google controls retention and deletion of the data it processes under the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. There is no separate analytics SDK beyond these advertising and consent services.

Importing, exporting and sharing

Formula packs contain function names, expressions, parameter names, folder labels and output-unit labels. They exclude your saved variable values and calculation history. A recipient may learn anything you include directly in a formula expression. History exports, shared calculations and PDF reports include the calculation information you choose to export. Android's file picker and share menu let you choose where these files go. Your selected email, messaging, storage or transfer service handles shared files under its own policies. Imports require confirmation before replacing formulas with matching names. Temporary shared packs may remain in the app cache until Android clears it or you clear app storage.

Support and platform services

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and attachments. We use them to respond and troubleshoot, retaining them as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the address above, subject to legal retention requirements. Do not send sensitive information unless you intend to share it. Android, Google Play and your chosen providers operate under their own terms and settings.

Security and changes

Local data uses Android app-private storage. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. For privacy questions contact Civilian Applications at applicationscivil.support@gmail.com.
