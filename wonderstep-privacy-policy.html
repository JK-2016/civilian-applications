WonderStep Privacy Policy

Developer: Civilian Applications
Effective date: 14 November 2026
Contact: applicationscivil.support@gmail.com

This policy covers WonderStep, Android package com.jk2016.minesweeper.

Gameplay and advertising

WonderStep does not require an account. Version 0.19.0 and later includes a Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the game screen, separate from the board and controls. Earlier versions through 0.18.2 contain no advertising SDK. Gameplay can be used offline, but ads and consent updates require a connection. Ads depend on availability and applicable consent. There are no in-app purchases.

Advertising data and privacy choices

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses, device and account identifiers (including advertising ID and app set ID where available), app and ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. The app does not request precise location, camera or microphone access, and does not send your saved scores or board contents to Civilian Applications servers. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Google User Messaging Platform checks whether consent is required before ad requests are allowed. Where applicable, a consent form is presented. Use Ad privacy options on the journey screen to reopen available privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Choices may affect personalization and available ads; non-personalized ads may still process data. Google controls retention and deletion of data it processes under its policies: Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. WonderStep includes no separate analytics SDK outside the advertising and consent services described here.

Information stored on your device

Journey progress, completed stages, stars, personal best times, game preferences and the current round are stored in app-private storage on your device so you can resume playing. Android cloud backup is disabled by the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app-private records. WonderStep has no developer-hosted account or account-deletion service.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot problems, and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the contact address above, subject to legal retention requirements. Do not include sensitive information unless you intend to share it.

Platform services

Android, Google Play and your email provider operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Android or Google Play may provide platform diagnostics under your device and account settings; WonderStep does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service beyond the advertising and consent services described above.

Security and changes

Game records use Android app-private storage. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. If the app changes its data practices, this policy and its effective date will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Civilian Applications at applicationscivil.support@gmail.com.
