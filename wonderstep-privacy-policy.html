WonderStep Privacy Policy

Developer: Civilian Applications
Effective date: 14 November 2026
Contact: applicationscivil.support@gmail.com

This policy covers WonderStep, Android package com.jk2016.minesweeper.

Offline gameplay

WonderStep works offline and does not require an account. The current app includes no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. It requests no internet, location, camera or microphone permissions. The app does not send game progress or personal information to Civilian Applications.

Information stored on your device

Journey progress, completed stages, stars, personal best times, game preferences and the current round are stored in app-private storage on your device so you can resume playing. Android cloud backup is disabled by the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app-private records. WonderStep has no developer-hosted account or account-deletion service.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot problems, and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the contact address above, subject to legal retention requirements. Do not include sensitive information unless you intend to share it.

Platform services

Android, Google Play and your email provider operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Android or Google Play may provide platform diagnostics under your device and account settings; WonderStep does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Security and changes

Game records use Android app-private storage. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. If the app changes its data practices, this policy and its effective date will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Civilian Applications at applicationscivil.support@gmail.com.
