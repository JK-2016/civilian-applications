Sudoku Standard Privacy Policy

Developer: Civilian Applications
Effective date: 14 November 2026
Contact: applicationscivil.support@gmail.com

This policy covers Sudoku Standard, Android package com.jk2016.sudoku. Version 0.16.0 is the offline, ad-free release. The ad-supported update described below is being prepared as version 0.17.0; it is not included in the previously supplied 0.16.0 bundle.

Offline gameplay

Sudoku puzzles, daily challenges, saved progress, personal bests and daily quotations work offline and do not require an account. The game does not upload your puzzle entries, notes, results or quotation-reading activity to Civilian Applications. There are no in-app purchases or a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

Home-screen advertising in the ad-supported update

The version 0.17.0 update is being prepared with a Google AdMob banner anchored at the bottom of the home and level-selection screen. It is separate from navigation and game controls, and is removed when you enter the puzzle board or daily-challenge selection screen. Ads require an internet connection and depend on consent, network availability and ad supply. Development builds use Google's test ad IDs; production ad IDs must be configured before the ad-supported release.

For ad-supported versions, Google's advertising and consent services may collect and share IP addresses, device identifiers including the advertising ID, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, measurement, service delivery and fraud prevention. IP addresses can be used to estimate approximate location. The app does not request precise GPS location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions, and does not send puzzle entries or local game records to the advertising SDK. The ad-supported app requests internet and network-state access.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks privacy requirements and displays a consent form where required. Ads are requested only when the consent service permits them. When required by that service, an “Ad privacy options” control on the home screen lets you revisit your choices. Your Android and Google advertising settings provide additional controls. Google's policies: Privacy Policy and Advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the current puzzle, your entered numbers and pencil notes, selected puzzle and difficulty, elapsed time, completion status, daily challenge progress and personal best times in Android app-private storage. These records let you resume puzzles and keep your results. Daily challenges are generated on your device; choosing a challenge date does not send that date to us.

The app's Android backup setting is disabled. Game records remain in app-private storage until you overwrite them, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Starting a new puzzle or restarting a round can replace the corresponding current-round records. Personal bests and saved daily records may remain until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. We do not hold a server-side copy or provide a cloud recovery service.

Daily quotations and device date

The quotation collection and author attributions are bundled with the app. A quote is selected using your device's local calendar date and time zone, and changes daily without an internet connection. The app does not send your date, time zone, selected quote or reading activity to us.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot problems, and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the contact address above, subject to legal retention requirements. Do not include sensitive information unless you intend to share it. Your email provider handles message delivery under its own privacy policy.

Your controls

You can remove locally stored puzzles, notes, progress and personal bests by clearing Sudoku Standard's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This removes local progress and cannot be reversed through a Civilian Applications account: Sudoku Standard has no developer-hosted account or account-deletion service.

Platform services

Android and Google Play operate under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process installation information or provide platform diagnostics according to your device and account settings. These platform services are separate from Sudoku Standard's offline game storage. The Google advertising and consent SDKs used in the ad-supported update are described above; the app has no additional analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask players for a name, email address or other personal details, and has no chat, public profiles or social-sharing features. Local game records are handled as described above. In ad-supported versions, Google's advertising and consent services may process the information described in the advertising section; local-only game storage does not mean the advertising SDK collects no data. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us to request review and deletion, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Security and changes

Game records use Android app-private storage. No storage method can guarantee absolute security or secure erasure on every device. If the app changes its data practices, we will update this policy and its effective date to describe the relevant release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Civilian Applications at applicationscivil.support@gmail.com.
