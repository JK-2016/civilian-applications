Sudoku Standard Privacy Policy

Developer: Civilian Applications
Effective date: 14 November 2026
Contact: applicationscivil.support@gmail.com

This policy covers Sudoku Standard, Android package com.jk2016.sudoku, including version 0.16.0.

Offline gameplay

Sudoku Standard works offline and does not require an account or sign-in. The current release includes no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs or developer-operated crash-reporting service. It requests no internet, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions. The app does not transmit your puzzle entries, progress or personal information to Civilian Applications or an app backend.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the current puzzle, your entered numbers and pencil notes, selected puzzle and difficulty, elapsed time, completion status, daily challenge progress and personal best times in Android app-private storage. These records let you resume puzzles and keep your results. Daily challenges are generated on your device; choosing a challenge date does not send that date to us.

The app's Android backup setting is disabled. Game records remain in app-private storage until you overwrite them, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Starting a new puzzle or restarting a round can replace the corresponding current-round records. Personal bests and saved daily records may remain until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. We do not hold a server-side copy or provide a cloud recovery service.

Daily quotations and device date

The quotation collection and author attributions are bundled with the app. A quote is selected using your device's local calendar date and time zone, and changes daily without an internet connection. The app does not send your date, time zone, selected quote or reading activity to us.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot problems, and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the contact address above, subject to legal retention requirements. Do not include sensitive information unless you intend to share it. Your email provider handles message delivery under its own privacy policy.

Your controls

You can remove locally stored puzzles, notes, progress and personal bests by clearing Sudoku Standard's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This removes local progress and cannot be reversed through a Civilian Applications account: Sudoku Standard has no developer-hosted account or account-deletion service.

Platform services

Android and Google Play operate under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process installation information or provide platform diagnostics according to your device and account settings. These platform services are separate from Sudoku Standard's offline game storage; the app does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Children's privacy

The current app does not ask players of any age for a name, age, email address or other personal details, and has no chat, public profiles or social-sharing features. Local game records are handled as described above. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us to request review and deletion, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Security and changes

Game records use Android app-private storage. No storage method can guarantee absolute security or secure erasure on every device. If the app changes its data practices, we will update this policy and its effective date to describe the relevant release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Civilian Applications at applicationscivil.support@gmail.com.
