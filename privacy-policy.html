DWG Pocket Privacy Policy

Developer: Civilian Applications
Effective date: 26 October 2026
Contact: applicationscivil.support@gmail.com

This policy describes DWG Pocket, including its ad-supported free edition and optional one-time Pro purchase. You do not need a DWG Pocket account to view files.

Your drawings and PDFs

You choose files through Android's document picker. DWG decoding, PDF viewing, password authentication and PDF export run on your device. We do not upload your drawings, PDF contents, filenames or document passwords to Civilian Applications servers or send them to advertisers. Local files can be viewed offline. Up to ten recently opened DWG previews are retained in the app-private cache to speed up reopening; file contents are checked before reuse, and Android can clear this temporary cache. These decoded previews may contain drawing geometry and text. Clear the app cache/storage in Android settings to remove them. A cloud storage provider selected in Android's picker may need a connection and handles its own transfers under its own policy.

Local storage and retention

The app stores appearance preferences, recent-document references, daily export counts and verified purchase information in its private storage. It creates temporary document, decoded-scene and PDF-preview files to perform the requested work. Password-protected PDFs may have an unencrypted working copy in the app's private cache while open. The app attempts to remove working files when finished and removes interrupted protected-PDF files on a subsequent app start. Android may retain other cache files until they are cleared. Document passwords are used for the current unlock operation and are not deliberately saved in preferences.

Advertising in the free edition

The free edition uses Google AdMob for a banner on the welcome screen. When a DWG is opened, including from recent files, the cache or another app, a separate bottom banner may appear once its preview is ready and is dismissed when you first interact with the drawing or its controls. We do not place advertisements over drawings or into exported PDFs. Advertising requires a connection and is subject to availability. Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses, device/account identifiers such as the advertising identifier, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. An IP address can be used to estimate approximate location. We do not request precise GPS location or provide your drawing contents to the advertising SDK. Where required, Google's consent service presents privacy choices before ads are requested. Use Ad privacy options in the app when available and your Android advertising controls to manage choices. The Pro edition does not request or display ads after its entitlement is recognized. Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Pro purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment information; we do not receive your card number or bank credentials. The app queries Google Play and keeps signed purchase information locally, including a purchase token, to verify Pro access, acknowledge purchases and restore access. An internet connection and the appropriate Google Play account may be required to buy, restore or recheck a purchase. Google Play purchases are subject to Google's payment and refund terms.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer your request and troubleshoot problems, and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. Do not send confidential drawings or passwords unless you intend to disclose them. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address above, subject to legal retention requirements.

Your controls

There is no DWG Pocket account to delete. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its private preferences, receipts and cache. A previously purchased Pro entitlement can be restored through Google Play. Exported PDFs and original files in Downloads, shared storage or a cloud provider are outside the app's private storage and must be managed separately. Advertising choices and data held by Google are controlled through Google's and Android's privacy tools.

Platform services and security

Google Play, Android, document providers and your email service operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK beyond the advertising, consent and billing services described above. Android or Google Play may provide platform diagnostics under your device/account settings. We use app-private storage for working files, but cannot guarantee absolute security or secure erasure on every device.

Children

DWG Pocket is a general productivity tool, not a service designed specifically for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated. For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, contact Civilian Applications at applicationscivil.support@gmail.com.
